Why HALBA exists

Somewhere in Kuwait right now, there's a boy who swims faster than anyone on his street. His parents know he's fast. But nobody has ever timed him, no club has ever seen him, and so he'll grow up, get a normal job, and the gift will quietly fade.

Michael Phelps had the same gift. The difference wasn't talent. Someone was watching.

Talent is spread evenly across the world. Opportunity is not. HALBA is our attempt to fix that for one region — the Middle East — one post at a time.

What HALBA is

Halba means the arena in Arabic. It's a place to show what you can do and be seen for it — football, swimming, running, gaming, singing, drawing, coding, anything. If you're good at it, it belongs here.

  • Post a photo or video of your skill.
  • Clap 👏 for what deserves it. One clap per person — it means something.
  • Comment with a purpose: Appreciate Suggest Ask — so fans can cheer, people who know the skill can help, and beginners can learn how it's done.
  • Teams — join the club you play for, or create one and bring your people in.

What HALBA is not

It's not a popularity contest. There's no algorithm deciding who matters and no reward for dancing to a trend. There is only the skill, and the people who recognise it.

Where this goes

Today HALBA is a stage. Tomorrow it's a ladder: city leaderboards, real competitions, and a path for academies, coaches and scouts to find the next champion before anyone else does. The kid on the dust pitch. The girl who outruns everyone at school. The gamer nobody takes seriously. They're all already here. They just haven't been seen yet.
